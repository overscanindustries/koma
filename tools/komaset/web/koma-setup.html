Set up a card

Koma lives on the memory card. With its file on the card, the camera runs Koma every time it starts. Take the card out and the camera is its usual self.

1. Get the Koma file

One file, the Koma module. On the card it must be called GRMOD.BIN.

2. Put it on the card

  1. Format the card in the camera (SETUP menu, FORMAT). This erases everything on the card, so copy your photos off first.
  2. Put the card in your computer and copy GRMOD.BIN to the top level of the card, not inside a folder.
  3. Put the card back in the camera and turn it on. Koma starts by itself.

Camera still on Ricoh's own software? Then it needs the Koma loader first, once. That step is a firmware flash: it rewrites the camera's own software, and a power cut or a wrong file during it can leave the camera unable to start.

Do it only by following the install guide step by step: docs/install.md. A camera that already has the loader skips this.

3. Check a card

After the camera has started with the card in and been switched off, put the card in your computer and choose the card itself. We read what Koma left there and say what happened. Nothing on the card is changed.
