CA-1 VBUS pulse decoder

Stock firmware reads one GPIO pin (VBUS sense, pin 0x38) and decodes pulse widths into shutter key events. A 500 ms high level is treated as a PC cable and kills the decoder until power-off.

Sources: analysis/vbus-shutter/README.md section 1 (classifier and state tables), section 2 (emulated arms), section 3 (CA-1 pulses 30 / 150 ms). Evidence class: STATIC and EMU; pulse timings from third-party descriptions. Never tried on a camera.
