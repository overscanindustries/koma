# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

### Added

- KOMA tab in the camera menu, with 5-tab, stock-plus-KOMA and GR II menu layouts.
- Snap focus distances from 1 m to infinity, and full-press snap.
- Auto-ISO ceiling and slowest-shutter limit.
- Highlight alert and focus peaking.
- Long exposures up to 240 s.
- 21 looks in IMG SET: ten black-and-white, six color, STANDARD, SELECT and three custom slots.
- Per-look editing: tone curve, contrast, key, shadow, highlight, filter, toning, saturation, warmth, tint, sharpness, grain and vignette.
- Looks on the ADJ lever, and look preview on the LCD.
- FAT32 SDHC and SDXC card support.
- Battery log.
- Koma app: setup, looks, camera settings, photo import, tethered shooting with live view, and webcam.
- Settings backup, restore and copying between cameras.
- Installation with Homebrew on macOS and pipx on Linux and Windows.

### Fixed

- The date and time are restored after the camera loses them with the battery out.
- The second tethered session no longer stalls for 10 to 20 seconds.
