# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

### Added

- KOMA camera menu and shooting options.
- Twenty-one looks with per-look editing and custom slots.
- Clock keeper and battery log.
- FAT32 SDHC and SDXC card support.
- Settings backup, restore and copying between cameras.
- Koma app for setup, settings and looks.
- Photo import and tethered shooting.
- Webcam support.
- macOS Homebrew and Linux and Windows pipx installation.
